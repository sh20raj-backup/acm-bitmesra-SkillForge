# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

SkillForge is a community project by ACM BIT Mesra contributors. We aim to make participation welcoming and constructive for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or affiliation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Give feedback that helps contributors improve.
- Respect privacy and do not publish private information.
- Accept differing opinions and focus on the project's goals.
- Follow repository policies and the terms of service of the platforms we use.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate disruption, spam, malicious submissions, and sharing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers or through the contact information listed on the ACM BIT Mesra organization's GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive details in public issues.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. They should explain moderation decisions where practical and handle reports respectfully.

This code of conduct applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project spaces used for SkillForge.
